God bless theUSA

A Rails engine holding every state, county, city and ZIP code of the United States.

Instructions to the surveyor

gem 'usa', '~> 0.5.0'

bin/rails g usa:install
bin/rails db:migrate

A database made from a schema dump carries the tables and none of the rows. bin/rails db:usa:seed fills it, and is safe to run again.
